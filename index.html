Meet Taylor

Read the story. Tap the pink words to find them in the Word Bank.

Taylor Swift is a talented singer and songwriter. She is also very creative. She is from the United States. She was born on December 13, 1989.

Taylor loves music. She plays the guitar and the piano. She writes her own songs. Her songs tell simple stories about love, friendship and life.

Her songs are often emotional. Her powerful performances connect with a large audience.

Taylor has a successful career. Her first album came out in 2006. Now she has millions of fans. Her fans are called Swifties.

Her journey is inspiring. She has passion for music and confidence on stage.

Look and think

  • Is Taylor talented?
  • What stories does she tell?
  • Is her career successful?
  • Why is her journey inspiring?

Teacher's Corner

How to use this page (A1 level)

1. Read aloud together. Ask the student to find and click the pink words. 2. Flip the Word Bank cards and ask the student to make a new sentence with each word. 3. Use the “Do you…?” questions for speaking practice. Ask follow-up questions: Why? When? Who with? 4. Finish with the quiz and the opinion sentences.

Extra speaking ideas

Describe a favourite album cover with colours. Say three things you like about Taylor's music. Ask your teacher: “Do you like Taylor Swift?” Practise: She is… She plays… She writes… She has… (third person “s”).
